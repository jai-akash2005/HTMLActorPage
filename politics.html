TAMILAGA VETTRI KAZHAGAM

History | Leader | performance

Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (transl. Victory Party of Tamilakam; abbr. TVK) is an Indian regional political party in the state of Tamil Nadu and the union territory of Puducherry. It was founded on 2 February 2024 by actor-turned-politician Vijay, who became its president. The headquarters of the party is in Chennai.

Orgins and foundation

During the early 2000s, actor Vijay had an extensive network of fan clubs across Tamil Nadu. Around 2009, these fan clubs were brought together under a structured "Narpani Iyakkam" (welfare association), aimed at organizing philanthropic and community service activities. On 26 July 2009, this initiative was formally launched as Vijay Makkal Iyakkam (transl. Vijay's People Movement) in Pudukkottai

The Iyakkam supported the AIADMK-led Alliance in the 2011 legislative assembly elections. Over the years, while the organization maintained a low-profile political stance, it began attracting attention as Vijay made politically resonant statements in his films and during public appearances. In October 2021, the organization contested the local body elections in Tamil Nadu independently and won 115 out of the 169 seats it contested.[8][9] Vijay also publicly criticized the Citizenship (Amendment) Act, 2019, calling it "unacceptable" as it would disrupt the social and religious harmony of the nation.

Information

Flag of TVK

On 2 February 2024, Vijay announced the launch of his political party, Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK), and stated its intent to contest the 2026 elections.[11][12] The formation of TVK marked the culmination of a long-standing political buildup from his fan base. Over the years, Vijay's fan clubs—reportedly numbering around 85,000 across Tamil Nadu, each with at least 25 members—had been organized under Vijay Makkal Iyakkam, a registered welfare association. These fan clubs have now transformed into a political cadre forming the backbone of TVK's grassroots structure.

Songs of TVK

“Unga Vijay” is a powerful Tamil anthem that gained prominence in 2024 and 2025 as part of actor Vijay’s transition into politics. The phrase itself—meaning “Your Vijay”—became a rallying cry for his supporters, symbolizing his promise to stand with the people. There are two notable versions of the song. The first is the official anthem of Vijay’s political party, Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK), titled “Unga Vijay, Na Varen,” which was released during the party’s second state conference in Madurai. This version features stirring lyrics and a bold declaration of leadership, emphasizing Vijay’s commitment to Tamil unity and progress. The second version, “Unga Vijay (Thalapathy Phonk),” is a remix by Orjunn, blending modern phonk beats with Vijay’s iconic persona. Released in late 2024, it quickly became popular among younger fans for its energetic style and digital flair. Together, these songs reflect both the political and cultural impact of Vijay’s evolving public image—one grounded in leadership, the other in pop culture resonance.

**The ideology song of Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK), led by actor Vijay, is titled “வாகை வாகை வாகை வெற்றி தமிழ் வாகை” and was released during the party’s first state conference in October 2024. It serves as a musical manifesto of TVK’s core values and vision.** This song is a stirring anthem that encapsulates the party’s commitment to Tamil pride, social justice, and inclusive governance. The lyrics emphasize themes like equality (*“பிறப்பொக்கும் எல்லா உயிருக்கும்”* – all lives are born equal), righteousness (*“அறத்தோடு வாழும் குலத்தோன்”* – one who lives with virtue), and the empowerment of the working class (*“உழைக்கும் இனத்தின் விளைச்சல்”* – the yield of the laboring community). It also celebrates Tamil heritage and positions Vijay as a leader born of the soil, ready to usher in a new era. Musically, the song blends traditional Tamil poetic rhythm with contemporary orchestration, creating an emotional and motivational tone. It was released alongside other symbolic elements like the party flag and anthem, reinforcing TVK’s identity as a movement rooted in cultural pride and political reform. The song’s release marked a significant moment in Vijay’s political journey, transforming his cinematic charisma into a voice for change. It’s not just a campaign jingle—it’s a declaration of purpose.
