Vijay

Vijay is an Indian film actor and playback singer who works predominantly in Tamil cinema. He has appeared in over 65 films and is one of the highest-paid actors in South India. Known for his charismatic screen presence and versatile acting skills, Vijay has a massive fan following and is often referred to as "Thalapathy" (commander) by his fans. Some of his notable films include "Ghilli," "Thuppakki," "Mersal," and "Master." In addition to his acting career, Vijay is also involved in philanthropic activities and has a significant influence on Tamil culture and cinema.
